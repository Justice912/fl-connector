## Decisions And Boundaries

- Reconstruction promises a faithful editable approximation, not recovery of Suno's hidden presets, mixer values, or original project file.
- Files stay local under `.data/reconstructions`; analysis is single-concurrency and retryable.
- MIDI is used only at sufficient confidence. Low-confidence and texture/vocal material remains aligned audio.
- Installed FL sounds are preferred. Missing tools are recommendations with official and license links; they are never downloaded automatically.
- System prerequisites require explicit consent and verified `winget` package metadata.
- The existing `flp.score.addNote(...)` Piano Roll writer remains the verified FL note-write surface.
- The current guide images are instructional interface maps, not captured FL Studio screenshots.
